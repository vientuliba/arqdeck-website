# ArqDeck source assets

Product and company facts are recorded in [product-reference.md](product-reference.md).

`images/` contains the optimized images used by the website. Original product photographs and supplied logo variants are retained in `reference/` for future editing. The original background-extracted Full Deck master is `full-deck-cutout.png`; it was supplied with the project and derived from the genuine Full Deck photograph in `reference/9b90d727-106f-4a28-a2ae-017b0b11a6d0.jpg`.

The redundant copy of that original photograph and the raw third-party catalog export were archived locally outside the repository during publication cleanup. The product reference retains the relevant facts, prices, contacts and team roles. The raw export contained third-party catalog navigation and organization details that are not part of the ArqDeck website.

Only the unmodified design-kit runtime files needed by the page are retained in `taste/`.
