# Website design

ArqDeck is a Latvian student company developing modular desktop controllers. Product facts, prices, team roles, contacts, production and delivery come from the supplied reference.

The site uses a dark charcoal/sage palette and IBM Plex typography, with genuine product photography as the main visual material. A lighter module section shows the separate physical controls. The Full Deck and Small Deck have their actual prices and included parts. The remaining sections cover public companion software, production/delivery, the verified team and contacts.

The existing Taste composition is retained: product-stage hero, split navigation, solid buttons, a large-name dark footer, and the iris-opens and letters-assemble moments. The runtime kit files are unchanged; unused demos, photographs, render engines and components were removed during repository cleanup.

Copy is concise and factual in Latvian and English. Headings use product/section names, and neither language uses em dashes. No compatibility lists, software features, testimonials, certificates, dimensions or other product claims were invented.

Three historical checker exceptions are retained in .taste-skill/waivers.json: the team roster uses text because real portraits are unavailable; its existing contact button is overlooked by a CTA heuristic; the inset iris overlay is generated by the unmodified kit itself.
